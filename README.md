# Necessary Roughness Analytics

## About the Project

In 2026, I decided to play fantasy football for the first time. My draft was messy and a little panicky, and I didn't fully understand what it took to manage a fantasy football team that faces new challenges every week.

Consequently, I lost my first two matchups and felt a bit overwhelmed by all the information. But that overwhelming amount of data also sparked my excitement and curiosity about football statistics. I quickly realized just how much data was available to help us make informed decisions about our fantasy teams each week!

From that curiosity and my natural appreciation for data, Necessary Roughness Analytics was born!

An easy-to-use fantasy football analytics dashboard designed to help beginners learn the game, make informed weekly decisions, and explore football statistics in a fun and approachable way. More experienced players can also dive into deeper analytics to gain additional insights.

Fun fact: My fantasy football team is called Necessary Roughness, and we are the champions of the world! 🏆

## Who Is This For?

Fantasy football beginners and experienced players alike! Whether you're just learning how to manage your first team or looking for deeper statistical insights, Necessary Roughness Analytics aims to make football data accessible, useful, and fun.
